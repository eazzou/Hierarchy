Study 03 / Gloock + Chivo

This experiment puts Gloock’s sharp, high-contrast serifs against Chivo’s sturdy sans-serif shapes. I used the same body font as study 01 so the heading change is easy to compare. Gloock stays at weight 400; size creates the heading hierarchy. The 4/3 ratio gives the headings more contrast.

Perfect fourth · 16px base · ratio 1.333
Size = 16 × ration; H1–H4 use steps 4, 3, 2 and 1. Body, emphasis and links use step 0.

H1 · Gloock · 50.57px / 3.1605rem · weight 400

The numbers behind the game

A final score tells us who won, but it does not explain everything that happened. Looking at the possessions, decisions and chances behind that score gives us a better picture of the game.

H2 · Gloock · 37.93px / 2.3704rem · weight 400

A closer look at performance

A player can have a strong game without putting up the biggest numbers. Efficiency, timing and the quality of each opportunity help explain how much a performance actually mattered.

H3 · Gloock · 28.44px / 1.7778rem · weight 400

Context changes the story

Numbers are more useful when we know what they represent. A change in opponents, playing time or team strategy can affect a result, even when the player is doing the same things.

H4 · Gloock · 21.33px / 1.3333rem · weight 400

Small details matter

A useful analysis connects the statistics to what happened on the field. Clear labels and readable text make it easier to follow the evidence and understand the conclusion.

Emphasis · 16px / 1rem · weight 700

A useful statistic needs context before it becomes a useful conclusion.

Body · 16px / 1rem · weight 400 · line height 1.7

Good typography makes a page easier to follow. Each heading signals a new level of information, while the paragraph text stays consistent so readers can focus on the content.
